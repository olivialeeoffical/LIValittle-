A little about me ♡

I'm Olivia — a creative, curious person who loves turning ideas into something people can see, feel, and connect with.

My interests live somewhere between psychology, visual storytelling, community, and the little details that make a brand feel human. I love exploring how creativity can bring people together and make important messages easier to connect with.

My style is a little nostalgic, a little whimsical, and always full of personality. I believe creativity doesn't have to be perfect to be meaningful.
